Why GreenNest

Rooted in the belief that everyone deserves a garden

GreenNest started in a small backyard with a few pots of tomatoes and too many questions about why half of them weren't growing. We built the shop we wished existed back then — one with honest product descriptions, fresh seed stock, and tools that actually last.

Today, we work with organic seed banks, local nurseries and small-batch soil makers to bring reliable, chemical-free gardening supplies to balconies, backyards and rooftops everywhere.
